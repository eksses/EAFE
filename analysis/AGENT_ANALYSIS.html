Conclusion: ✅ AUDIT.md CORRECT. v7.1 explicitly documents this as a correction to v7. Vanilla LivingEntity.travel uses 0.99 for X/Z and 0.98 for Y. Unified 0.99 causes vertical position drift over long distances.

PHYS-002 HIGH v7 lift coefficient formula has no physical basis
EAFE-v7.md lines 64-67: C_L = cos(θ) × min(1.0, ||v||² / 1.0) v_y,lift = v_y,gravity + (d_y × √(v_x² + v_z²) × 0.1 × C_L) EAFE-v7.1.md lines 22-29 (two scenarios): Scenario A (nose-up, φ<0): Δv_y = v_h × (-sin φ) × 0.04 Scenario B (nose-down, φ>0): Δv_x = (v_x/v_h) × (-v_y) × cos²φ × 0.1 EAFE-v7.1.md line 16, 32: Passive wing lift cos²φ × 0.06
Conclusion: ✅ AUDIT.md CORRECT. v7 uses a single arbitrary formula (C_L saturates at speed=1.0, 0.1 scaling undocumented). v7.1 supersedes with decomposed model matching vanilla decompilation: separate nose-up (0.04) and nose-down (0.1) coefficients plus passive wing lift term. mimo-v2.5-free provided thorough worked evidence confirming this.

Corrections to AUDIT.md

EDGE-001 — Nether Void Weight (w₄=200)

AUDIT.md says: "Reduce to w₄ = 1.0 or document that high weight ensures immediate path rerouting"

Correction: DO NOT REDUCE. The high weight is a safety feature. mimo-v2.5-free's worked example proves that reducing to 1.0 makes void nodes score LOWER than lava-proximate nodes, causing the pathfinder to PREFER void-adjacent routes.

With w₄=200: void node H=200, lava node H=100 → void avoided ✓ With w₄=1.0: void node H=1, lava node H=100 → void PREFERRED ✗

Action: Update AUDIT.md to note the high weight is intentional safety design, not a bug.

EDGE-003 — Portal Blockage Timeout

AUDIT.md says: "v7 uses 60-tick timeout. Vanilla has 80-tick cooldown. Risk of pulling bot out before shift completes."

Correction: The 80-tick cooldown applies to re-entry after a successful dimension shift. v7's 60-tick timeout measures time from portal entry to dimension-shift packet arrival — different metrics. However, 60 ticks (3s) may still be too aggressive for laggy servers.

Action: Update AUDIT.md to clarify the distinction. Recommend increasing timeout to 100-120 ticks with documentation note.
