Critical Issues Corrected

🔥 HIGH-IMPACT CORRECTIONS

1. EDGE-001 — Nether Void Weight (w₄=200)

AUDIT.md said: "Reduce w₄=200 to w₄=1.0"

ERROR. The 200 weight ensures void-avoidance dominates over lava (H=200 vs H=100 at d=1). Reducing to 1.0 makes void nodes PREFERRED (H=1 vs H=100 for lava). This is a SAFETY FEATURE, not a bug.

2. EDGE-003 — Portal Timeout (60 ticks)

AUDIT.md said: "60t vs vanilla 80t — bot pulled out before shift completes"

PARTIAL ERROR. The 80t cooldown applies to RE-ENTRY after successful shift. v7's 60t measures time from entry to dimension-shift packet arrival (different metric). However, 60t (3s) is aggressive for high-latency servers.
